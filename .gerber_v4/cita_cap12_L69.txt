THE INNOVATION The next time you want somebody to do something for you, touch him softly on the arm as you ask him to do it. You will be amazed to find that more people will respond positively when you touch them than when you don’t.

Again, to apply this to your business, you or your salespeople should make a point of touching each customer on the elbow, arm, or back some time during the sales process. You will find, as our clients have found, that there will be a measurable increase in sales.
